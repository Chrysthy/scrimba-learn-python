Step 1: Calculating the service charge

A percentage like 15% means "15 out of 100." To use it in a calculation, you first convert it to a decimal by dividing by 100:

15 / 100 = 0.15

Then multiply that decimal by the cost to get the dollar amount. Say your expense was $200:

200 \* 0.15 = 30

A 15% service charge on a $200 expense is $30.

Python lets you do both steps in one line:

service_charge_total = cost \* service_charge / 100
